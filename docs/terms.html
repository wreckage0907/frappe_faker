3. Intended use

Frappe Faker is meant for test, demo, staging and development sites. It inserts real records into the site it runs on, and in fast-insert mode it skips document hooks and validations. You are responsible for choosing where to run it. Back up any site that holds data you care about first, and use the batch rollback to remove what it created.

4. Generated data

Generated records are produced by a language model and are meant to be fictional. Any resemblance to real people, companies or transactions is coincidental. They may also be inaccurate, inconsistent or unsuitable for your purpose. Do not use generated data as real business, financial, legal or personal information.
